# Real demo session: copy these prompts in order

Use a fresh ASI:One conversation with @agenticgamemaster. These are inputs, not
claimed results. Inspect the actual responses before recording.

## 1. Create the scene

Create a short tabletop adventure in Saltmere, a coastal town where every spell
permanently consumes one memory from its caster. The lighthouse has gone dark.
Include one quest, one NPC with a secret, and one dynamic event. Keep the scene
compact. The party has not accepted any job or made any promises yet.

## 2. Check player agency

We ask the NPC what reward they offer and what risks the job involves. We have
not accepted the job, promised help, cast a spell, or handed over a memory.
Continue only this negotiation and leave our decision pending.

## 4. Inspect the GM record

## What to verify

- The scene uses the supplied world rule.
- Negotiation does not become an accepted quest or a paid memory cost.
- Status commands do not advance the story.
- /status hides story details; /gm-status shows the stored GM record.

If any check fails, keep the real response as a bug report. Do not describe a
failed check as successful. These owner tests do not replace external trials.

## Evidence to collect from each assigned external tester

- Assignment reference and date.
- Confirmation that they used ASI:One.
- Their actual prompt and relevant response, with permission to share.
- Their original Trick / Treat / Feedback note.
- For the selected improvement: issue, change/commit, and retest result.

Feedback written about another team's project is outgoing feedback. It is not
evidence that another team tested Agentic Game Master.
