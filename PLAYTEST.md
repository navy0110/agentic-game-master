## Task

Ask for a short quest in your own fictional world, then negotiate with an NPC
without accepting the offer. Check whether the agent preserves your world rules
and leaves the choice to you.

### 1. Prepare a scene

Use your own world, or this example:

> Create a 10-minute adventure for two novice heroes in a coastal town. Every
> spell permanently consumes a memory. An archivist needs help locating a missing
> bell. Include a quest with objectives, an NPC and a dynamic event. No player has
> accepted any offer. Use system-neutral mechanics.

### 2. Test player choice

In the SAME conversation:

> The heroes ask the archivist for terms, but accept no bargain and make no
> promise. Describe the NPC's response and leave the decision open.

### 3. Check the record

Send `/status` for a spoiler-safe summary. As the GM, `/gm-status` shows the full
record INCLUDING SECRETS. Confirm the offer is still pending. Status commands
should not increment the turn or call the model.

Optional: explicitly accept only one limited task, then inspect the record again.

## What to tell us

- Where did you hesitate, get stuck, or wait unexpectedly?
- Was the quest playable without rewriting it? What was missing?
- Did the agent contradict a world rule or make a decision for your players?
- What ONE change would most help you prepare your next session?

Write your own observations. Do not use suggested or copied feedback.
For official Stranger Builds credit, use the project assigned through Trick or
Feedback and submit your note there. Direct tests are useful but do not replace
the required assigned participant trials. No reciprocal votes or invented usage.

## Expectations

Story generation can take around a minute. Commands are faster. Free-form outputs
may include GM secrets. No live game-engine control or cross-session import yet.
Use fictional data; story context is processed by ASI and stored in Agentverse.
