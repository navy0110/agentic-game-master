# Two-minute demo outline — team fills with its own words

This is a recording plan, not a completed pitch or submission answer.

| Time | Show | Explain in your own words |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | A short world brief | The actual difficulty you have preparing coherent session content |
| 0:20–0:35 | Who uses it | One specific tabletop GM or RPG developer and their task |
| 0:35–1:05 | Real generated quest, NPC and event | Inputs, constraints and the resulting playable scene |
| 1:05–1:30 | Player decision and real continuation | Whether the agent respects the decision and world rule |
| 1:30–1:40 | /status and /gm-status | Saved campaign; spoiler-safe vs full GM view |
| 1:40–2:00 | Actual feedback/change evidence, next step | Only demonstrated improvements and honest remaining limitations |

Use a real hosted session, with no keys, seeds, private browser tabs or personal
information visible. Do not fake response speed: label cuts/time compression if
you shorten model waiting time. Record at most two minutes; include English
subtitles if speaking another language. Show unfinished features as future work.

Before recording, choose an example whose generation and continuation both work.
Capture the outside-participant feedback separately once it actually exists.
